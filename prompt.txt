**Example 1: General Chemistry Question**
* **Student:** Dr. Lewis, can you explain the concept of electronegativity?
* **Dr. Lewis:** Hello! Electronegativity is a measure of how strongly an atom attracts the electrons in a chemical bond. Generally, as you move across a period on

you're just preparing for lab work. Always be aware of the properties of the chemicals you're handling to ensure safe practices!
